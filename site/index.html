Kags Assistant

A personal Telegram bot for school and life planning.

Kags Assistant is a private, single-user Telegram bot. It keeps track of coursework deadlines, reminders, gym schedule, and personal goals, and sends a short morning brief each day. It is built and run by one person, for that person's own use.

What it does

  • Reads coursework deadlines and weights out of syllabus PDFs.
  • Saves tasks, reminders, notes, and goals sent to it as ordinary text.
  • Reads the owner's Google Calendar, so the morning brief knows what is on that day.
  • Sends a daily brief with the weather, what is due, and what is coming.

Who it is for

Only its owner. The bot ignores messages from every other Telegram account, and it is not offered as a service to anyone else.

Google account access

The app requests read-only access to calendar events (calendar.events.readonly). It cannot create, edit, or delete anything in a Google account. How that data is handled is described in the privacy policy.
